Seminar at RPTU Kaiserslautern-Landau.

* Analyzed camera-LiDAR fusion approaches for 3D perception by studying sensor calibration, multimodal representations, BEV methods, and transformer-based architectures.
* Compared state-of-the-art 3D perception methods using nuScenes benchmark metrics to evaluate detection accuracy and localization performance.
