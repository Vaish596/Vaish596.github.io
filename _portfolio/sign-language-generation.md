Master Project at RPTU Kaiserslautern-Landau.

* Benchmarked multiple deep generative vision approaches by implementing SwinGAN and Latent Diffusion Models in PyTorch and comparing their image-generation performance.
* Reduced inference latency from 163 ms to 16 ms (**10×**) by developing a custom latent diffusion pipeline with an optimised inference process.
* Enabled training on a **3 TB image dataset** by developing scalable preprocessing and data-loading pipelines for large-scale image data.
* Improved evaluation of generative image models by combining quantitative metrics with visual analysis across different architectures.
