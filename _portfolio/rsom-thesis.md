Master Thesis at the German Cancer Research Center (DKFZ).

* Developed an end-to-end deep learning pipeline in PyTorch Lightning for medical image restoration of Photoacoustic mesoscopy (RSOM) data.
* Benchmarked three classes of reconstruction methods by implementing and comparing CNN and Transformer based regression models, GAN, and diffusion-based architectures across the same imaging task.
* Established quantitative assessment of reconstruction quality by evaluating image and medical domain metrics.
* Analysed model failure cases across different image structures and vessel sizes to understand reconstruction limitations.
* Compared image-domain and data-domain restoration formulations and their effect on reconstruction quality.
* Improved reproducibility of reconstruction experiments with structured experiment tracking, hyperparameter management, and model comparison using Weights & Biases.
