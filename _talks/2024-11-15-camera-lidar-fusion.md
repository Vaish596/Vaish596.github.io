Presented a seminar on camera-LiDAR fusion approaches for 3D perception in autonomous driving, covering:

* Sensor calibration and multimodal representations
* Bird's-eye-view (BEV) methods
* Transformer-based architectures
* Comparison of state-of-the-art 3D perception methods using nuScenes benchmark metrics to evaluate detection accuracy and localization performance.
